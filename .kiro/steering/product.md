# Plus1 Rewards Platform

Plus1 Rewards is a healthcare funding platform that enables members to earn cashback toward medical cover plans through shopping at partner stores.

## Core Business Model

- Members shop at partner stores and earn cashback (3-40% of purchase)
- Cashback funds monthly medical cover plans (R390, R500, R750 tiers)
- Partners provide cashback and pay monthly invoices
- Agents recruit partners and earn 1% commission
- Policy Providers receive funded policy data
- Admin manages the entire ecosystem

## Cashback Distribution

When a partner offers cashback:
- 1% → System fee
- 1% → Agent commission
- Remainder → Member's cover plan

## Key Features

- Multi-plan cover management with overflow handling
- Offline transaction processing with sync queue
- QR code-based member identification
- Invoice generation and payment tracking
- Commission calculation for agents
- Policy export to insurance providers
- Real-time cashback calculation and allocation

## User Roles

- **Members**: Shop and earn cashback toward cover plans
- **Partners**: Shops offering cashback on purchases
- **Agents**: Recruit partners and earn commission
- **Policy Providers**: Medical cover providers
- **Admin**: System control center

## Platform Status

- Progressive Web Application (PWA) with offline capabilities
- Supabase backend with PostgreSQL database
- Real-time updates and subscriptions
- Mobile-first responsive design
